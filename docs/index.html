PCG tools for Unreal Engine 5.8

Turn a blockout into a dressed scene in minutes.

Three procedural dressing tools for designers and environment artists. Draw a spline, drop a box or pick a mesh, and PropMaster fills it with your meshes, clusters, moss and decals. Every result is seeded and editable.

Spline

Paths, walls, fences and pipes along a spline, or scatter filling a closed area. Stack meshes into walls, follow landscape layers, keep off tagged roads.

Spline guide →

Volume

Dress a room's floors, walls and ceilings, or the outside of rocks and props. Pick faces by the way surfaces face, pile meshes out of any surface.

Volume guide →

Mesh Cover

Grow moss, vines, debris and decals over meshes already in the level, by top, side and bottom, facing a direction or along tagged splines.
